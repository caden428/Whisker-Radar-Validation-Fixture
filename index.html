Detection Geometry & Validation Plan
Characterization only - no acceptance criteria applied. Positions are unrestricted; fixture travel and reflector-route protections remain active.
Heading: 0° down on the map, negative left, positive right, plus or minus 180° up. Beam overlap is informational only.
System qualification treats both radar channels as one DUT module: detect through 304.8 mm from the nearest DUT edge, unscored through 609.6 mm, and no detection beyond 609.6 mm.
0° is straight ahead toward decreasing Y. Positive angles are fixture-right and negative angles are fixture-left. The filter applies only to automatically generated plans.
Both plans face toward decreasing Y and follow the manual's tapered detection lobe. Test 10.1 fills the complete 12-inch area through its boundary. The guard band applies only to Test 10.2, which fills the surrounding negative-test band through the configured outer depth.
Changes apply to the next characterization run. Apply is unavailable until the active run finishes so its plan cannot be replaced.

System-Level DUT Edge Barriers
Distances are measured from the nearest physical DUT edge. The grey outer edge and red start describe the same barrier and stay synchronized. Defaults: green through 12 in, grey from 12 to 24 in, red beyond 24 in.
Full DUT coverage means the accessible front, left, and right sides only. Rear coverage is never generated because it is outside the fixture's physical motion requirement.

Error Code Reference
CodeDescriptionSuggested Corrective Action
ERR001Motor FaultCheck TMC UART wiring and motor connector on the affected axis; power-cycle the SKR board.
ERR002Home Switch FailureVerify XSTOP/YSTOP wiring and switch actuation; confirm homing direction in printer.cfg.
ERR003Driver OfflineCheck the MicroUSB/UART link between the Pi and the SKR board; reseat the cable and restart Klipper.
ERR004Raspberry Pi Communication LostCheck network/IP configuration and that the Pi is powered; confirm the Moonraker service is running.
ERR005Position TimeoutCheck for mechanical binding; verify configured speed/accel don't exceed the motor's real capability.
ERR006Travel Limit ExceededVerify the commanded coordinate and configured axis limits. Reflector Z is reset automatically before each trigger.
ERR007Reflector Trigger TimeoutCheck the REFLECTOR_SPIN macro, Z motor/driver, and Moonraker/Klipper logs. X/Y reached the requested test position before this error.
ERR008DUT Collision ZoneThe requested endpoint or straight-line XY path touches or crosses the selected DUT footprint. Choose a safe point or move around the DUT.
